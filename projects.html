RESEARCH SYSTEM · ACTIVE

Warren

A research pipeline for automated vulnerability discovery, built around the thesis's central idea: the discovery engine is subordinate to a non-bypassable enforcement layer. Authorization, scope, rate/blast-radius, verification, kill-switch, and a tamper-evident audit log are implemented as gates the discovery logic can't route around.

Public description only — this is a research artifact evaluated on self-owned, reproducible targets. No private findings or target-specific detail are published.
