What I work on

Automation that can find security flaws is powerful and dual-use. My work focuses on the part most tools skip: building the rails — authorization gating, scope enforcement, blast-radius limits, kill-switches, and tamper-evident audit — so that a discovery system cannot act outside what it's allowed to do. This is the subject of my Master's thesis and the research system behind it.
